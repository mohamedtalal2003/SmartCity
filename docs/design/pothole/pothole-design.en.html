Pothole Service Design

Last updated: 14 October 2026, after the decision review. Scenarios are numbered with the letter "P" so they do not get mixed up with the Work Order numbers.

  1. 1. Scenarios ✓
  2. 2. The endpoints
  3. 3. The domain

The Pothole service knows the street: where the potholes are, how bad they are, when they were seen, and their photos. It decides if each detection is a new pothole or an open pothole we saw before, and it calculates the estimate. The repair steps themselves live only inside Work Order. The pothole knows only one thing from there: the ticket was closed, and why.

Decisions

  1. The service is Onion-shaped, and the logic lives in the domain: the matching decision and closing the pothole. The PostGIS SQL lives in the infrastructure. Decided
  2. Storage uses two tables: a main record with the current state of the pothole, and observations with every time it was seen, corrected or checked. Observations are only added, never edited. Decided
  3. The estimate is calculated inside Pothole by its own estimator, PotholeEstimator.Estimate(severity, stage, areaM2, depthCm, prices), when a pothole is created or when its severity or stage changes. The prices come from the config and are passed in. There is no shared calculator: Inventory and Building will each have their own estimator, because each kind of thing is repaired in a different way. The estimate goes out inside PotholeSaved. Decided
  4. A pothole has only two states: Open and Closed, with Merged later. Closed is final, and a pothole never opens again. Decided
  5. Each pothole has only one ticket in its whole life. If a pothole is seen in the same place after it was closed, it is a new pothole with a new ticket. Decided
  6. The repair steps (assigned, crew arrived, paused, waiting for confirmation) live only in Work Order. Pothole has no rule that depends on them. Decided
  7. Pothole keeps the ticket state in a display-only column (repair_status), so the map and lists can filter by it. No rule inside Pothole looks at this column. Decided
  8. The post-repair check belongs to Work Order, because the list is "tickets waiting for confirmation". Pothole saves the result of each verification pass as an observation, so the "after" photo exists. Decided
  9. Names: observations for the observation records, row_version for the counter that stops two people from editing the pothole at the same moment, and ticket_sequence for the order number in ticket messages. The word version alone is not used. Decided
  10. Pothole is the model, and Inventory and Building will copy the same shape. But the 10 October decisions apply only to potholes for now, and the other two services stay as they are. Decided
  11. A crack and a pothole within 3 meters are one damage record, not two. The record has a damage type (damage_type) and a stage, and both only move forward as the damage grows, from a crack to a pothole. They never go back, unless an officer corrects them. Each observation also keeps its own damage type, so the timeline shows the crack turning into a pothole. Decided

Table shapes

potholes
  id, location, damage_type, district_id, neighborhood_id, road_name,
  status, closed_reason, closed_at, duplicate_of_id,
  current_severity, current_stage, area_m2, depth_cm, confidence,
  est_materials, est_labor, est_equipment, est_total, source, sightings_count,
  work_order_id, repair_status, repair_status_seq,
  first_seen_at, last_seen_at, row_version

observations
  id, pothole_id, source_detection_id, kind,
  damage_type, severity, stage, area_m2, depth_cm, confidence,
  verification_result, image_url, notes, seen_at, created_by

recurring_spots
  id, location, district, pothole_ids, pothole_count,
  first_seen_at, last_seen_at, computed_at

admin_areas
  id, name, level, parent_id, geometry
  • The row_version column in the main record stops two writers from writing to the same pothole at the same moment.
  • The closed_reason column: repaired, false, duplicate, or resolved another way. duplicate_of_id is the other pothole's id when the reason is "duplicate".
  • The work_order_id, repair_status and repair_status_seq columns are a display-only copy of Work Order messages (P6).
  • The kind column in observations: a detection from the AI, an addition by the officer, a manual correction, or a verification pass. verification_result is filled only in a verification pass: gone, still there, or not visible.
  • The recurring_spots table is filled by the job for potholes that come back in the same place (P17).
  • The admin_areas table holds the districts (ilçe) and neighborhoods (mahalle) of Istanbul, loaded once from OpenStreetMap GeoJSON (admin_level 6 and 8). When a pothole is created, it gets district_id and neighborhood_id from a point-in-polygon check (Intersects).

Pothole state

A pothole has two states: Open and Closed. Closed is final and comes with a reason. Any move not in the table is not allowed.

Allowed moves

FromToCauseCondition
None (new pothole)OpenA new detection with no open pothole within 3 meters, or the officer added itThe place is inside Istanbul, and AI confidence is 50% or more Default. The confidence condition does not apply to an officer's addition
OpenClosed (repaired)A message that the ticket became CompletedThe ticket belongs to this pothole
OpenClosed (false)The ticket was cancelled as "false", or a DetectionDismissed messageSame pothole
OpenClosed (duplicate)The ticket was cancelled as "duplicate"The message has the other pothole's id, and the other one is open
OpenClosed (resolved another way)The ticket was cancelled for this reason, for example the whole street was repavedSame pothole
OpenMergedThe officer merged it into another potholeDeferred

Not allowed

  • Any move after Closed or Merged. If the officer closed it by mistake, they add the pothole by hand (P1), and it becomes a new pothole. Decided
  • A pothole going back to Open for any reason. Decided
  • Changing the state with a direct command from the officer. A pothole closes only when its ticket closes. Decided

If the condition is not met

  • If it is a command from a person: rejected with 409.
  • If it is a message: ignored, and a warning is logged. We do not retry, because sending the same message again will not change the result.

Repair status for display

This is not the pothole state. It is a copy of the ticket state, stored in repair_status so the map and lists can filter and sort by it in one query. The screen shows it like this:

Ticket stateShown on screen
No ticketNothing
PendingWaiting for a crew
Assigned and EnRouteCrew on the way
OnSiteCrew working
PausedPaused
AwaitingReviewRepaired, waiting for confirmation
RejectedCrew coming back to repair again

If Work Order adds a new state later, the column will be filled with it and nothing in Pothole will break. At most, the screen needs to learn how to show it.

Out-of-order messages

After the new split, Pothole rules no longer depend on the order of Work Order messages. Only the order of the display-only column is left, and that is one line.

  • Closing the pothole: happens from one final message (the ticket was closed). Closed is closed, so if the message arrives twice or late, it makes no difference.
  • The display-only column: every message from Work Order has ticket_sequence, which is the ticket's own counter (its row_version). Pothole overwrites repair_status only if the pothole is open and the new number is bigger than repair_status_seq. If the pothole is closed, any message from Work Order is ignored. Contract change
  • The other direction, from Pothole to Work Order: if an old PotholeSaved message from the AI arrives after the officer's correction, the priority may go back to a wrong value. The fix is for the message to carry the pothole's row_version, and Work Order drops the older one. Deferred
public void ApplyRepairStatus(string status, long ticketSequence)
{
    if (Status != PotholeStatus.Open) return;        // closed: ignore
    if (ticketSequence <= RepairStatusSeq) return; // older message
    RepairStatus = status;
    RepairStatusSeq = ticketSequence;
}

This code has no rules: no "allowed" and "not allowed". It only makes sure the screen shows the latest state.

Officer commands

The officer can add a pothole, correct its data, and merge two potholes. But the officer cannot change its state by hand.

P1 The officer adds a pothole by hand

Why
A citizen reported it, or the officer saw it in person, and no vehicle has passed over it yet. Also if the officer closed a ticket by mistake.
Command
Add pothole: the place, the photo link (from POST /api/uploads), the rough severity or size, the source (citizen report or officer), and notes. Contract change
Rejected if
The place is outside Istanbul, or the photo is not from our blob storage, or both severity and size are missing.
Result
An Open pothole, the estimate is calculated, PotholeSaved goes out, and the ticket flows as usual.
Special cases
  • There is an open pothole within 3 meters: the report is added to it as an observation, and the response returns its id.
  • Next to a pothole Closed as "false" less than 30 days ago: the addition goes through as usual and makes a new pothole, because the 30-day rule is for the AI only. Decided
  • No size: the estimate is calculated from the severity, and the screen shows it as "approximate" because area_m2 is empty. There is no column for this. Decided
  • The AI confidence condition does not apply here, because the officer saw it with their own eyes.
  • Double click: the Idempotency-Key protects against it.

P2 The officer corrects a pothole's data

Why
The AI measured the size or the severity wrong.
Command
Correction: the severity, the stage, the area, or the depth, and the reason. Contract change
Rejected if
The pothole is Closed or Merged, or nothing changed, or the reason is missing.
Result
A new observation of kind "manual correction", the main record is updated, the estimate is calculated again, and PotholeSaved goes out with Reason = correction.
Priority
  • The correction always goes through, even if it lowers the severity, and the ticket priority changes with it. Decided
  • Unless the officer had changed the priority by hand in Work Order: their decision there stays. Decided
  • The rule "if the severity goes down, nothing changes" is for the AI only.

P3 The officer merges two potholes Deferred

Why
The system made two potholes that are really one pothole. This can happen even from one vehicle, if it photographed the same pothole in two photos one after the other.
Until merge is built
The officer cancels the duplicate pothole's ticket as "duplicate", with the other pothole's id. The pothole becomes Closed and duplicate_of_id points to the other one. Decided
Later
A real merge command: the pothole becomes Merged, and its observations move to the remaining pothole.

P4 Changing the pothole state by hand: not allowed

Why not
A pothole closes when its ticket closes. If the officer closed it from Pothole while the ticket is still open, we would have two places saying opposite things.
Instead
  • To close it: close the ticket in Work Order with the right reason, and the pothole closes by itself.
  • To correct data: scenario P2.

Things the system does by itself

Detections come from the AI, the state comes from Work Order, and Pothole publishes only when something important changes.

P5 A new detection arrived

Source
RawDetectionReceived from the AI: the place, the size, the depth, the confidence, and the photo.
What happens
  • We look for an open pothole within 3 meters Default. If we find one, a new observation. If not, a new pothole.
  • We calculate the estimate, and publish if something important changed (P9).
Special cases
  • The same message came twice: the inbox stops the duplicate.
  • Next to a pothole Closed as "false" less than 30 days ago Default: only an observation on the closed pothole, so the same mistake does not create a pothole every day.
  • Next to a pothole Closed for any other reason, or "false" more than 30 days ago: a new pothole (P8).
  • AI confidence below 50% Default: if there is an open pothole nearby, an observation. Otherwise it is ignored and logged.
  • The message is incomplete, for example no depth: rejected and sent to _error. Proposal
  • Two detections of the same pothole at the same moment: we may get two potholes. The fix (the lock on the grid cells) is deferred, and the officer closes the duplicate as "duplicate". Deferred

P6 Work Order messages arrived

Listens to
WorkOrderCreated and WorkOrderStatusChanged. Every message has SourceEntityId (the pothole id) and ticket_sequence. Contract change
What it does
  • If the pothole is open: update the display-only column (work_order_id and repair_status) if ticket_sequence is bigger than the one it has. The message that closes the pothole first writes the last repair status, then closes.
  • If the ticket became Completed: the pothole is Closed as "repaired".
  • If the ticket became Cancelled: the pothole is Closed with the cancel reason (false, or duplicate with the other pothole's id, or resolved another way).
  • Any other state: only the display-only column, and the pothole stays Open.
Special cases
  • The pothole is already closed: the message is fully ignored, because any message after closing is old. Decided
  • Two writers on the same pothole at the same moment: row_version rejects the second one, and MassTransit retries it.

P7 The officer dismissed a detection in a manual district

Source
DetectionDismissed from Work Order. Contract change
What happens
The pothole becomes Closed as "false", and the 30-day rule starts.
Special cases
The message has the pothole id, and the pothole always exists, because it is created before the officer even sees the detection. So we do not need to keep dismissed detections in a separate table. Decided

P8 A pothole seen where a closed pothole was

What happens
Matching looks only at open potholes. So a place with a Closed pothole is empty for matching, and the detection makes a new pothole with a new ticket. Decided
The exception
A pothole closed as "false" less than 30 days ago: only an observation on it, from the AI. An officer's manual addition still makes a new pothole as usual. Decided
Why
Each pothole has only one ticket, so a repaired pothole never opens again. If the street breaks again in the same place, this shows up in the stats of places where potholes come back (P17).
Special cases
The pothole is still open and its ticket is waiting for confirmation, and the AI saw it on a normal pass: only an observation. The confirmation decision comes from the results of verification passes (P10), not from normal detections.

P9 When to publish PotholeSaved

Publish when
  • A new pothole was created.
  • The severity or the stage changed.
  • The officer added it or corrected its data.
Do not publish when
The pothole was seen again and nothing changed. Only an observation, so Work Order does not drown in useless messages.
In the message
The main record, the estimate, and a Reason field that says why the message was sent: new, changed, or corrected. Contract change

P10 Post-repair check

List owner
Work Order. The list is the tickets in state AwaitingReview, meaning the crew sent the proof. Decided
Steps
  • Work Order publishes VerificationRequested when the ticket enters AwaitingReview, and VerificationEnded when it leaves it. The message has the place. Contract change
  • Vehicle Communication keeps a copy of the list, and sends it to the vehicles so the camera takes photos at exactly these places. This is the first time the server sends to the vehicle over MQTT.
  • The AI answers every photo from these places with a RepairVerificationObserved message: gone, still there, or not visible. Contract change
  • Work Order decides: two "gone" results after the proof time Default, and the ticket becomes Completed. "Still there": only an alert to the officer, who decides to reject the work or wait. Decided Automatic rejection is Deferred
  • Pothole saves each result as an observation of kind "verification pass", with the photo. The newest "gone" photo is the "after" photo on the portal.
Special cases
  • "Not visible" (a parked car, night, or rain): changes nothing.
  • Photos taken before the crew sent the proof do not count.
  • The officer closed the ticket before the vehicle: VerificationEnded goes out, and the place leaves the list.
  • No vehicle passed for 14 days Default: the officer assigns an inspection crew to the same ticket. Decided
Open
When does the vehicle get the list (before it enters the district, or when it gets close)? And if the vehicle is offline, does it get the full list when it comes back? Open

Why it helps

The same error table as in the Work Order design. Examples from Pothole:
